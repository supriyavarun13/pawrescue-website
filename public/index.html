What We Do

Our rescue programs

Every program in our Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud instance tracks beneficiaries, goals, and outcomes — ensuring every animal gets the right care.

Emergency Rescue
24/7 rapid response for animals in immediate danger. Each case opened as a Service Delivery record in Nonprofit Cloud with full care tracking.

Foster Network
A managed network of 320 foster homes. Volunteer relationships tracked in Nonprofit Cloud — matching animals to compatible carers automatically.
